# Frequently Asked Questions – Print on Demand Services

## Ordering & Production

### How does Print on Demand work?
When you place an order, our system automatically sends the design to our production facility. Your item is printed specially for you, which helps us reduce waste and offer a wider variety of unique designs.

### How long does production take?
Typically, items take **2–5 business days** to be printed and processed before they are shipped. During peak holidays, production times may be slightly longer.

### Can I change or cancel my order?
Because production begins almost immediately after an order is placed, we can only accept cancellations or changes within **2 hours** of purchase.

---

## Shipping & Delivery

### How long will it take to receive my order?
Shipping times depend on your location. 
- **Domestic (US):** 3–7 business days after production.
- **International:** 10–20 business days depending on customs.

### Do you provide tracking?
Yes! As soon as your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number so you can follow its journey to your door.

### My order is marked as delivered but I haven't received it.
Please wait 24 hours as carriers sometimes mark items as delivered prematurely. If it still hasn't arrived, check with neighbors or your local post office before contacting our support.

---

## Quality & Sizing

### How do I know which size to pick?
We provide a **Size Guide** on every product page. We recommend measuring a favorite garment you already own and comparing it to our charts for the best fit.

### What printing method do you use?
Most of our apparel is printed using **DTG (Direct to Garment)** technology, which allows for high-detail prints and a soft feel that integrates with the fabric.

---

## Returns & Exchanges

### What is your return policy?
Since every item is made to order, we do not typically accept returns or exchanges for sizing issues. However, if your item arrives **damaged** or with a **printing error**, we will send a replacement at no cost to you.

### How do I report a problem with my order?
Please email our support team at [support@yourstore.com] with your order number and clear photos of the issue within **14 days** of receiving your package.
